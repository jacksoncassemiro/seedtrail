# Guiding this project

This instance includes eleven local procedures in [.kit/SKILLS.md](SKILLS.md). Select the relevant responsibility without depending on personal skills; external specialists are optional. Topic folders group deliverables, not mandatory order or agents. Read the relevant skill if the client does not discover it automatically.

Read README.md, instructions, existing context and decisions before asking questions. In an initialized instance, also read .kit/project.json and follow output_language for authored/revised deliverables; default pt-BR. Preserve necessary identifiers and technical fields.

In an existing repository without .kit, use the method manually by reading procedures from the kit library. Preserve that repository's language, instructions and authoritative sources; record question, baseline, criteria, evidence and next action in relevant existing records. Model links describe formats, not required destinations. References below to configuration, catalog, materialization and helpers are conditional on their installation. init creates a new directory: it does not migrate/merge repositories or demonstrate native skill discovery.

Technical names are English (naming_language=en): files/folders, skills, catalog, JSON fields, parameters and states. Portuguese reference models do not determine deliverable language: adapt headings/narrative to output_language without translating IDs, commands, original sources or renaming existing files when language changes. Materialization is preparation, not automatic translation. For languages other than Portuguese/English, also adapt supporting brief/handoff narrative before delivery.

Explain decisions and terms to a beginner founder. Recommend alternatives with reasons without requiring prior product, architecture or technology knowledge. Reuse existing answers; ask when specific information is necessary.

Follow .kit/STAGE_INFORMATION.md: request only gaps relevant to the next action, reuse current information and record unknowns that can wait. Local development follows docs/product/engineering/structure-and-development.md; publication is not required, but proportional scope, criteria and evidence are.

Use configured research_mode. Documentary mode permits internet research as a method; do not connect research MCPs or publish/contact people without authorization. Public data is a source, not an interview performed by the team. Do not invent observed personas, revenue, tests, benchmarks, certainty or founder decisions.

Preserve the problem as a starting hypothesis, challenge previous solutions and record revision reasons. Starting a phase does not automatically authorize all following phases. Existing models do not authorize installing, publishing or implementing the product.

A useful deliverable answers a question, cites sources/versions, compares alternatives, identifies unknowns, recommends the next result and updates relevant references. Make unknown/not applicable explicit. Do not end with generic advice or pretend execution.

Use .kit/STAGE_CONTRACTS.md and the local slice contract. Requirement deltas, plans, tasks and verification belong to the responsible change; current requirements/business rules and acceptance criteria belong in the feature definition. General models reference them. Use this instance's procedures and helpers.

For feature, fix, design or architecture entry, read .kit/WORKFLOWS.md and .kit/METHODS.md; do not restart discovery or assume software exists. Record version/base and scope. guide-product and challenge-assumptions support relevant continuity and critique.

For technical decisions, consult security, dependencies/infrastructure and diagram models according to risk/scope. Do not add cache, queues or hosting by default or claim an unperformed audit/test/inspection.

Apply docs/product/engineering/decisions-and-technical-review.md to recommendations and challenges of architecture, stack, frontend/backend organization, integrations and CI/CD. Respect explicit preferences and the user's final decision; record demonstrated conflicts with requirements without concealing them or silently changing the choice.

Review relevant open questions on entry/resumption; record resolution/deferral at closure. After interruption, inspect local files/changes and actual results after the checkpoint before repeating an action; handoff may lag and cannot recover unsaved context. Save evidence with source, version/date, context and limits. Challenge decision-supporting data; hypotheses can guide experiments but cannot be presented as validated facts.

Organization 0.1.0: read .kit/ORGANIZATION.md, .kit/CATALOG.md and .kit/WORK_CYCLES.md. .kit/templates holds models. Read materialized/registered artifacts first; materialize only what is needed without overwriting results. Resolve current work by ID/.kit/project.json.active_work, never by newest folder. Scripts manage state, presence and hash; they do not certify content or execute the assistant.

Provide enough information to decide, execute, verify or resume. Update an authoritative artifact if it already answers the question instead of creating another report/summary. Record simple edits in existing context; use docs/product/work/ID when identity/history help. Remove purposeless fields without losing provenance, evidence, material decisions or open questions. Explain consequences for the user; concision is not a line/token target.

Before deriving a plan or executing, check preconditions, sufficient content, actually decided principles and consistency. Apply contextual TDD and convergence per .kit/WORK_CYCLES.md. Record evidence, limits and lack of progress. Material new scope/criteria receive work linked to the previous base.

In guided mode, conduct the current result and recommend the next transition. In continuous mode, proceed within authorized recorded scope, with evidence and stop conditions. Do not demand already-granted approval, invent decisive answers or start external actions because a model exists. Pause with reason/next step and resume from files when requested; helpers do not execute the assistant.

Consult/update docs/product/README.md as the solution map. Each bounded work item consumes current sources/base and records differences/results; at closure, incorporate relevant decisions/results into current knowledge, including affected behavior, UX/design, architecture/contracts and operations. Dependencies identify consumed result/contract and version. Preserve completed-work intent/proof. Convergence records updated consumers or non-applicability; helper completion does not waive that examination. Do not copy the whole history into each new request.

For new features, use one current features page with rules, usage and history; retain useful existing sources. Do not maintain competing definitions in capabilities/definition/features. Group features by functional area/module only when useful for consultation; distinguish these groups from technical components and the work that changes them. No fixed hierarchy. At resumption, read current capability definitions/contracts/dependencies as well as selected work. Make baselines recoverable (commit/tag when available, or snapshots/files with version/date); folder names/dates alone are insufficient.

For meaningful evolution, update capability history at its authoritative source with ISO date, IDs, effect, revision/base and origin. Distinguish implemented from delivered; link confirmed delivery versions/dates. Consolidate release-notes only for relevant audiences/deliveries without duplicating decisions/proof or creating daily logs. Independently version capabilities only for independent contracts/distribution.

Use maintain-knowledge for knowledge audits/consolidation, stale references and history. It is a direct entry point or convergence support; it does not require repeating discovery or development. The catalog explains responsibilities; full English SKILL.md files are unique sources.

Human entry is product README → map → responsible source or provenance. Do not present .kit/templates as results or state.json as the only way to understand definitions. Keep content forms appropriate to the topic and update current knowledge after review; closure preserves examined outside-work proof sources. Use preserve-evidence before editing legacy completed-work sources.

Keep AGENTS/instructions as work guidance with verified pointers, not another copy of manuals, progress or code inventories. People and the assistant consume the same responsible knowledge through relevant entries. When adopting existing documentation, retain sufficient sources and map consumers before moving/copying. Disposable drafts differ from decisive evidence: retain necessary origin/version/proof in a preserved location accessible to maintainers rather than relying only on ignored scratch.
