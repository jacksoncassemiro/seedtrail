# Selected references and limits

Conceptual references consulted through October 7, 2026. Procedures/skills were authored for this kit; referenced projects are not dependencies. Before incorporating third-party material, check license/version, identify reused content and preserve applicable notices.

Popularity does not prove usefulness. Selection considers question, provenance, instructions, license, available tools and verifiable results. Incorporate a tool only for a need existing procedures cannot resolve.

Additional references used as selective practices: [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/) for verifiable requirements, [threat modeling](https://owasp.org/www-community/Threat_Modeling) for risks/boundaries, and [dependency management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Vulnerable_Dependency_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html) for inventory/triage. Original procedures reference these sources without reproducing entire standards or granting certification.

Continuity and coverage: [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec/blob/main/docs/concepts.md) distinguishes domains/requirements/scenarios; [arc42](https://docs.arc42.org/section-5/) admits selective decomposition; [C4](https://c4model.com/abstractions) distinguishes technical abstractions. [BMad project context](https://docs.bmad-method.org/existing-codebases/set-and-maintain-project-context/) and dzhng [write-docs](https://github.com/dzhng/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/write-docs/SKILL.md) help avoid redundant context and code mirrors. Our adaptation lives in organization/maintain-knowledge, without imposing these taxonomies or importing frameworks. For effects by delivery, [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [Towncrier](https://towncrier.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial.html); short history in existing sources/work, without requiring extra fragments. Consulted on 2026-10-06.

Research and human reading: [Docs as Code](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/docs-as-code/) informs reviewable/versioned files; [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) distinguishes reader needs without requiring four empty directories. The [Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/) separates exploration/convergence with people and testing; desk research is not equivalent. [Cognitive walkthroughs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cognitive-walkthroughs/) informed task inspection using hypothetical personas, without observed participants. Adaptations and limits appear in methods/guide; no text, diagrams or templates copied.

Prototypes and components: [GOV.UK prototyping guidance](https://prototype-kit.service.gov.uk/prototyping/) reinforces choosing a representation by the question; [GOV.UK Design System contribution criteria](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/community/contribution-criteria/) inform component usefulness, usage context and maintenance. Selective use in flows, DESIGN and review: state simulations/limits, exercise content/width variations and link contracts to implementation. We do not adopt its visual system, tool, government publishing process or a requirement for a formal design system. Consulted on 2026-10-07.
