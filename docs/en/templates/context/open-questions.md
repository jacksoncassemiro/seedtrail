# Open questions and advancement conditions

Status: initial register; creating this file resolves no items.

Types include product question, missing evidence, conflict, technical choice, execution/verification or maintenance. States: open, investigating, resolved with evidence/decision, deferred with reason or superseded.

On entry/resumption, review items affecting current delivery. Every question is not a blocker for the entire product. Distinguish next-action blockers from hypotheses testable in reversible bets.

At closure, register new questions and resolve only with relevant evidence/decision references. Deferred items are not complete. User-dependent items may stay open while appropriate documentary research/prototyping proceeds with stated limits.

Before handoff/release, check relevant questions and who accepts risks/limits. This register does not approve automatically or invent deadlines/owners.
