# Idea and context

Status: discovery start; fill from founder/sources without inferring decisions.

## Context to recover for the next decision

Reuse existing answers/sources; this list is not a mandatory questionnaire. Unknowns that do not change the next action can wait, with a review reason/condition. Capacity and budget precede commitments that depend on them; their absence alone need not block independent desk research.

- Idea/problem and investigation motive.
- Potentially affected people/contexts; user versus buyer.
- Known current alternatives, with sources.
- Previous material: hypothesis, draft or evidence? What may be revised?
- Capacity, time, budget, constraints and technical experience.
- User availability and research mode.
- What this bet is not trying to solve.

## Output

Opportunity summary, prioritized unknowns and first investigation plan. Reference decisions/dates and consumer documents.
