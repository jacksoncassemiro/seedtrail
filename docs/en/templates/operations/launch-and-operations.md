# Pilot, launch and operations

Status: not released.

Plan pilot audience/limits, onboarding, documentation, support, observability, relevant recovery/export/retention, costs and owners. Define value signals, window and measurement sources.

Campaign: audience, supported promise, channel, message, calendar, spending limit and metric. No fictitious stories/revenue.

Triage: origin, context, expected/observed, impact, audience and change link. Public material needs appropriate content; links do not grant access to internal material.

Output: release conditions, operational guide, learning plan and actual results when collected.
