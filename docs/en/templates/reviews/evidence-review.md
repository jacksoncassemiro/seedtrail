# Data challenge and evidence preservation

Status: protocol; no evidence audit performed here.

## For a material conclusion

1. Record the claim and supported decision.
2. Find original source, author, date/version and context; verify actual support.
3. Distinguish verifiable data, reports, interpretations, estimates and hypotheses; state uncertainty without demanding impossible certainty.
4. Seek counterevidence/plausible alternatives; compare segments/populations/periods without combining incompatible data.
5. Do not count duplicates/republications as independent sources or use another AI's text as factual proof.
6. Separate missing data from absence of a problem; record conditions invalidating the conclusion.
7. Keep conclusions bounded or open precise questions/experiments. Preserve superseded-source history and update consumers.

## What to save

Research register: ID, URL/path, author, relevant dates, context/segment, faithful synthesis, limit, related claim/hypothesis and next decision. When permitted/useful, preserve a short original excerpt or identified snapshot/export; do not copy restricted material or full articles by default.

Technical results: environment/version, command/procedure, expected/observed, relevant log/artifact, limits and consumers. Screenshots prove an interface under those conditions, not all behavior.

Public research can reveal alternatives, episodes and signals. It does not automatically prove segment usability, payment or retention. Estimates remain estimates in tables.

## Conclusion

Connect claims to sources in docs/product/research/desk-research.md, questions in docs/product/context/open-questions.md and responsible decisions. Do not eliminate every hypothesis: make them visible, challengeable and testable.
