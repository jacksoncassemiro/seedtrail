# Desk research and evidence register

Define question, scope/stop condition and consuming decision. Record claims/evidence with useful IDs and verdict (supported, refuted, inconclusive), original source, date/version, method, excerpt/synthesis and limits. On resumption, read saved state; do not repeat sufficient research or claim inaccessible sources were verified.

Status: protocol; filling this model performs no research.

## Preparation

Define question, audience/context, terms/synonyms, sources, relevant period and decision-changing signal. Research the problem and substitutes, not just the imagined solution's name.

## Sources

Official competitor documentation for current capabilities; papers/institutional data for context; public issues/discussions/reviews for reported episodes. Find original quotation/statistic sources; record conflicts of interest and limits.

Official sources do not prove satisfaction. Public comments are indirect reports with limited selection, authenticity and coverage; they do not automatically represent our audience. Stars, upvotes and result counts are not demand/revenue.

## Procedure

1. Compare mechanisms, manual alternatives and doing nothing.
2. Record URL, author, data/publication/access dates and context.
3. Distinguish verifiable fact, report, inference and hypothesis.
4. Do not count the same person/data as independent signals.
5. Challenge counterevidence, language/region/segment and missing coverage.
6. Synthesize patterns and explain confidence/limits without invented percentages.
7. Derive alternatives, experiments, provisional decisions and consumers.

## What can advance

Alternative maps, candidate opportunities, hypothetical requirements, prototypes and assumption-based estimates. Do not claim demonstrated usability, payment, retention or audience pain magnitude.

## Future experiment

Hypothesis, audience/material, task/comparison, measurement, investment limit and continue/adjust/stop criteria. Without people available, investigate relevant documentation/technical proof and record precisely what was not tested.
