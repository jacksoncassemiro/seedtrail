# Experiment, prototype and MVP

Status: model, no experiment executed. A prototype represents interaction; an MVP is a minimal delivery testing a relevant hypothesis under defined conditions. They can support each other but are not synonyms.

For the current bet, record question/hypothesis, potential counterevidence, simple alternative, candidate audience/context, smallest feasible test, authorized cost/time, procedure and decision criteria set before observing results. Do not impose arbitrary metrics/sample sizes on every product. Without participants, keep desk research/technical proof and explicit demand/usage validation limits.

| Result field | Content |
|---|---|
| Method, base and date | Document/prototype/manual operation/code; version/environment |
| Observed | Facts/sources separate from interpretation |
| Metric | Definition, origin, window/denominator and limitations; unknown without data |
| Conclusion | Continue, adjust, investigate or stop the bet; reason |
| Next consumer | Problem/value, UX, scope, roadmap or new change |

The first MVP prioritizes the central hypothesis and test-blocking risks. Do not include the entire future vision to fill feature categories. Relevant pilot security, accessibility and integrity cannot be discarded under the MVP label. Local demonstrations may address technical risk; they do not prove willingness to pay or retention.
