# Business and capacity

Status: to author. Inputs: problem/value, research and founder capacity.

Assess user/buyer/segment, accessible channels, revenue/pricing model, development/operation/support costs, people/resources/partners and adoption risks.

Market estimates need sources, method, reachable segment and limits; do not fill TAM/CAC/revenue without data. Effort estimates need ranges, assumptions and capacity, not fabricated schedules.

Output: feasibility scenarios, bet limit, main unknown, business experiment and review/stop condition. Use Lean Canvas/Value Proposition Canvas when useful without duplicating information.

Use [capacity, costs and evolution](../engineering/capacity-costs-and-evolution.md) to connect founder resources, MVP/pilot and sustainability. Record initial budget, recurring ceiling and available time/capacity; unknowns do not become unlimited resources. Technical decisions/roadmap must respect the selected scenario.
