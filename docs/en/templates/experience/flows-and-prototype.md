# UX, flows and prototype

Status: to author. Inputs: problem, rules and candidate audience.

Map starting situation, role, steps, information, decision/action, result and resumption. Compare relevant alternatives and comprehension/effort/risk tradeoffs. Storyboards and Mermaid can clarify behavior.

Represent applicable normal, empty, error, loading, restricted-access and recovery states. Define audience-appropriate accessibility/responsiveness.

Choose prototypes by what they test: storyboard, wireframe, navigation, landing page or manual operation. Identify the question and what the representation makes observable: content, appearance, navigation/states or operation. State what is real, simulated or omitted and the conclusions this permits; do not automatically increase fidelity in every dimension. Heuristic/AI review is not user testing.

Output: flows/states, proposed alternative, test protocol, results if performed and affected rules/documents. Prototypes are not automatically MVPs.

Apply the [review procedure](design-and-accessibility-review.md) for available material. Link accessibility to criteria/version/evidence; mentioning WCAG does not demonstrate compliance.
