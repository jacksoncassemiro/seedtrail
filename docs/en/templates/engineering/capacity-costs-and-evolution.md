# Capacity, costs and MVP evolution

Status: model; budget, load and team remain unknown. Consumers: feasibility, technologies, architecture, roadmap and operations.

## Available resources

Record founder money/time investment capacity, distinguishing availability from authorized spending limits. Gather initial budget, monthly operating ceiling, sustainability horizon, available hours, experience, team/hiring and existing tools. Unspecified fields remain unknown; do not assume unlimited or free investment.

Without supplied values, compare explicitly assumed scenarios without financial commitments or fixed deadlines. At the relevant decision, request only recommendation-changing information. Estimates do not authorize installations/purchases.

## Total cost and maintenance capacity

Include as relevant: discovery/design, construction/hiring, infrastructure, database/storage/traffic, paid integrations, domains/licenses, observability, backups, tests, dependency updates, support, fixes, security and future migration. Separate initial, recurring and variable costs; record currency, relevant taxes/exchange rates, date and official sources. Founder time has a capacity cost without cash outlay.

Free plans have limits/conditions and are not permanent zero-cost guarantees. Estimates need ranges/assumptions. Consider lower cash expenditure with greater operational effort without declaring it better before maintenance capacity is known.

## Comparable scenarios

Fill only decision-useful scenarios:

| Scenario | Question/outcome and scope | Capacity and initial/recurring cost | Limits/maintenance | Evidence/evolution condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Experiment/prototype | Test a specific hypothesis; manual or navigable | Estimate with sources/assumptions | Not an operable product | Learning needed for implementation investment |
| MVP/pilot | Smallest delivery testing value/usage with relevant quality | Estimate by budget/team | Bounded audience/load/features | Observed result and relevant technical/operational limit |
| Next version | Result/requirement-justified expansion | Required increment/sustainability | Migration, support and risks | Confirmed advancement criteria/capacity |

Do not define a “final product” as definitive architecture: vision guides choices, evolution follows learning. Compare reuse, build, integrate or operate manually as relevant. Reducing scope/deferring capacity may beat MVP complexity; essential usage controls remain necessary.

## Proportional scalability

Separate necessary initial capacity, plausible growth and hypothetical growth. Record active users/concurrency, data/files, critical operations, latency/availability and relevant sources/assumptions. Account count is not concurrent load.

Identify expected bottlenecks and observation methods; choose relevant tests/proofs. Review conditions need grounded metrics/limits, such as unit cost, latency, saturation, errors or operational effort. Without grounded measures/targets, record hypotheses instead of numeric triggers.

Cache, queues, separate services and horizontal scaling need justified need/cost. Document evolution path, migration effort, portability/export and hard-to-reverse decisions without building future infrastructure early.

## Output and decision

Recommend a scenario fitting known resources, user choices/limits, assumptions, open questions, required investment and advance/reduce/stop conditions. Link [technical challenge](decisions-and-technical-review.md) and [feasibility](../business/feasibility.md), without duplicating an authoritative budget elsewhere.
