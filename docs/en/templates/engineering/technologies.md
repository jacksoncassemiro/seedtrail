# Technology selection

Status: to investigate; no approved stack.

Inputs: requirements/constraints, architecture, capacity and platform. Define criteria before comparing languages, frameworks, databases, authentication, hosting, libraries and services.

Consult current official documentation, maintenance, support/versioning, licenses, cost, portability and operational needs. Compare enough options to reveal tradeoffs, including the simplest alternative.

| Decision | Criteria/assumptions | Options/sources | Recommendation/reason | Risk/review condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | To define | — | Not yet decided | — |

Use technical proof when material uncertainty cannot be resolved documentarily. Selecting technology does not imply a performed benchmark/test. Verify versions before recording; costs need assumptions.

Output: proposed/decided stack with tradeoffs and remaining questions. Founders need explained consequences/recommendations rather than choosing by name.

Follow [technical recommendation, challenge and decision](decisions-and-technical-review.md): final choice may differ from recommendation. Record preference, choice, rationale and consequences; update plans/structure and demonstrated requirement conflicts. Without a recorded choice, the stack stays proposed.
