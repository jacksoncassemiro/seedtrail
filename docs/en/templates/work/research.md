# Bounded investigation

Status: model, no investigation executed.

Question, origin/base, hypothesis and potential counterevidence. Method/sources and stop condition. Per claim: source/version/date, context, finding, limit and verdict. Synthesize alternatives, counterevidence, recommendation and unknowns. Public sources are not our own interviews; results may be inconclusive. Preserve recoverable evidence and identify decisions/consumers.
