# Decision and continuity

Status: model, no decision made.

Question/origin, alternatives, evidence/limits, assistant recommendation and owner's choice. State: proposed, decided, deferred or superseded. Identify reason, risks, affected consumers, review condition and next step. Preserve history; opinions do not become observations and unknowns do not become approval.
