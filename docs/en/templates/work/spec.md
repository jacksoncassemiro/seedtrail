# Change specification

Status: model, behavior neither approved nor implemented.

Identify origin/base, problem/outcome, audience/context, scope/non-goals. Requirements use stable IDs and scenarios: context → action → observable result. Relate state.json criteria to requirements; cover relevant access, data, failures and edge cases. Identify consumed foundations, conflicts/questions and actual decisions. Distinguish verifiable criteria from post-launch metrics.

Consumers: [plan](plan.md), [tasks](tasks.md) and [verification](verification.md). Do not copy another canonical spec. Rule changes update sources/consumers with reasons.

## Differences and dependencies

| ID/affected capability | Current source and base/version | Proposed difference (add/modify/remove) | Criterion/consumer |
|---|---|---|---|

| Needed result/contract | Source/work and version | Why/at which stage | Required condition/observed availability | Open question/action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep relevant relationships only. Defined proposals may support prototypes without implying implemented integrations. Before execution, check necessary dependencies and record mock/proof limits. Capabilities are solution parts; work is the change defining/modifying them. Do not create packages per file or stage transition.

## Consumed definition and documentation

Link current rules/contracts and the consultation page/section, when available. Identify readers and affected consumers: usage guidance, reference, UX/design, technical or operational docs. Change specs are not another complete feature manual; retain IDs and consolidate after verification. Distinguish available versus proposed state.

Record change criteria and relevant regressions here, linked to the consumed definition. Requirements, business rules and acceptance scenarios that remain applicable belong on the feature page; incorporate new behavior there with state/provenance without presenting a decision as implementation. Test results, version/environment and proofs belong to change verification. Criteria IDs in state.json organize work rather than replacing the feature contract.
