# Product roadmap and execution management

Status: to author; no approved dates.

A roadmap orders bets/outcomes; a project plan organizes scope, owners, capacity, dependencies, costs, milestones and risks; a backlog decomposes verifiable work. They are not the same list.

Define now/next bet/later, hypotheses, priorities and review conditions. Estimates need assumptions, ranges and capacity; do not schedule every idea as an obligation.

Output: first-bet outcome, investment limit, dependencies and change-follow-up method. Reassess through research/results instead of automatically executing old schedules.

Connect MVP/increments to [capacity/cost/evolution scenarios](../engineering/capacity-costs-and-evolution.md). Distinguish long-term vision from delivery commitments; expansions need recorded outcome/requirement, maintenance capacity and advancement conditions.
