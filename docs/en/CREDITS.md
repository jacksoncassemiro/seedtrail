# Provenance, credits and license scope

This is an independent project: scripts, templates and eleven local procedures were authored for this kit with AI assistant support. It was not created by forking/cloning an upstream. It is not an official version or endorsement by GitHub, DeepSeek, Anthropic, OpenAI or referenced authors.

## License and future incorporation

Original framework files use [MIT](../../LICENSE): use, modification and distribution, including commercial use, with preserved license/copyright notices. Standard license text remains English; reading documentation is available in English and Portuguese. Collective attribution does not determine future contributors' division of rights; authors/holders and contributions must remain identifiable during repository maintenance.

The license covers the kit, not data, external sources, brands or the product developed in an instance. Instances receive the framework license in .kit/LICENSE without imposing MIT on the entire product. Credits do not replace permission to redistribute third-party material.

Before incorporating code, templates, prompts, images or text from another project, record source, version/commit, license, included excerpts/files, changes and required notices. Without sufficient permission, retain only a reference or author an original implementation. A future fork must identify upstream, base and differences. Do not attribute third-party copies to the kit.

## Review and limits

During 0.1.0 preparation, the eleven SKILL.md files, local templates/helpers and provenance statements were inspected. This inspection identified no copied/redistributed upstream package or content without declared provenance. Existing references were preserved and cycle/method references added. It is a review of included material, without exhaustive comparison against all public texts; it is not absolute proof of no similarity or a legal authorship determination.

Primary guidance: [GitHub repository licensing](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/licensing-a-repository), [MIT](https://choosealicense.com/licenses/mit/). Publishing and granting reuse rights are related but distinct decisions; publish only the generic package, without project data/credentials.

Continuity also uses conceptual distinctions from [OpenSpec](https://github.com/Fission-AI/OpenSpec/blob/main/docs/overview.md) (current knowledge versus changes), [ADRs](https://adr.github.io/) (rationale and supersession), [arc42](https://arc42.org/overview/) (relevant views) and [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) (reader needs). Their templates, texts and executors were not incorporated; adaptation and limits are described in [knowledge and evolution](KNOWLEDGE.md).

Capability history/delivery notes also use [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and [Towncrier](https://towncrier.readthedocs.io/en/stable/tutorial.html) as conceptual references without incorporating templates, commands or runtimes.

For organization, selective/recursive [arc42 decomposition](https://docs.arc42.org/section-5/) and technical [C4 abstractions](https://c4model.com/abstractions) were conceptual references. Audit/consolidation coverage also considered [BMad context](https://docs.bmad-method.org/existing-codebases/set-and-maintain-project-context/) and dzhng [write-docs](https://github.com/dzhng/skills/blob/main/skills/engineering/write-docs/SKILL.md). No reproduction of these authors' templates/procedures; our choices and limits appear in the coverage review.

Research and human reading: [Docs as Code](https://www.writethedocs.org/guide/docs-as-code/) informs reviewable/versioned files; [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) distinguishes reader needs without requiring four empty directories. The [Double Diamond](https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/the-double-diamond/) separates exploration/convergence with people and testing; desk research is not equivalent. [Cognitive walkthroughs](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/cognitive-walkthroughs/) informed task inspection using hypothetical personas, without observed participants. Adaptations and limits appear in methods/guide; no text, diagrams or templates copied.

For shared human/AI consultation, [AGENTS.md](https://agents.md/), [Kiro practices](https://kiro.dev/docs/specs/best-practices/), [Cursor rules](https://cursor.com/docs/rules) and [Cline Memory Bank](https://docs.cline.bot/best-practices/memory-bank) were conceptual references. No upstream prose, instructions or executors copied; feature documentation explains contributions and limits of this adaptation.
