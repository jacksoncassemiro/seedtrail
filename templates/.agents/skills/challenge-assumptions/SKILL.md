# Review assumptions

Locally authored. Conceptual references: [Contrarian](https://github.com/aaddrick/contrarian) and [Idea Validator](https://github.com/averychernin/idea-validator). No upstream bundles/agents, subagent initiation or official releases.

## Inputs and scope

Identify decision, problem, audience/context, sources/versions, alternatives and known capacity. Use existing context before asking. Reviewing a color/flow does not require full market assessment.

Present the proposal in its strongest form before critique. Challenge the current version without applying superseded rules as current.

## Procedure

- Extract assumptions and distinguish observation, report, inference, preference and unknown.
- For conflict/decisive claims, use [evidence review](../../../docs/product/reviews/evidence-review.md). Relate conclusions/sources to research records; open/resolve relevant questions without turning unknowns into facts.
- Test concrete failure scenarios: situation, affected person, cause, consequence and alternative. Do not manufacture disagreement.
- Challenge a simpler alternative, current practice and doing nothing.
- When relevant to the bet, distinguish demand (problem, benefit, adoption/payment signals; public data is indicative), execution (who can deliver/maintain, capacity/cost/dependencies; no invented experience/budget), and distribution (reaching users/buyers, channel hypotheses; no fabricated CAC/conversion).
- For technical/visual decisions, examine specific implications and needed proof/rendered review. Documentary critique does not replace execution.
- For architecture, stack, frontend/backend structure, integrations or CI/CD, read [technical decisions/review](../../../docs/product/engineering/decisions-and-technical-review.md). Derive criteria from product/capacity, challenge assistant recommendations too, preserve final user choice and record demonstrated incompatibilities. Bounded technical decisions do not require full commercial analysis.
- For each material concern: source, trigger, consequence, supporting/opposing evidence, mitigation and what would change the conclusion.
- For budget, maintenance or scale, use [capacity/costs/evolution](../../../docs/product/engineering/capacity-costs-and-evolution.md); distinguish MVP/future vision and known resources. For existing projects, follow [assessment](../../../docs/product/engineering/existing-project-assessment.md), with repository/execution evidence and limits; do not recommend rewrites by preference.
- Recommend continuing a bounded bet, revising points, obtaining specific evidence or not investing in the current proposal. Unknowns are not automatic approval/rejection.

## Output

Record in the [review model](../../../docs/product/reviews/critical-review.md), with a section per review or identified file when needed. Results are bounded advice, not viability certification.

Link observations to affected decisions/documents. Correct demonstrated contradictions; hypothetical risks remain hypotheses. Do not silently change direction/scope or implement a solution just to close critique.

For UX/UI, challenge task/material through [design/accessibility review](../../../docs/product/experience/design-and-accessibility-review.md); record principle/criterion, status, evidence and verification. Architecture/market assumption critique does not replace interface review.

## Organization and results

Read authoritative sources and any selected work relevant to this request. For a simple edit, preserve existing records; do not create work/state or load process guides just to apply the skill. Consult [organization](../../../.kit/ORGANIZATION.md) when destinations/authority need clarification, [catalog](../../../.kit/CATALOG.md) when selecting/materializing a missing artifact, and [cycles](../../../.kit/WORK_CYCLES.md) when managing work state, resumption or preserved evidence. Models guide format, not observed results; materialize only what is needed and preserve existing knowledge/proofs.
