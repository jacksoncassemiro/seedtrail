# Evolve product

Read delivery/limits, audience, decisions and available evidence. Use [operations/launch](../../../docs/product/operations/launch-and-operations.md), [roadmap](../../../docs/product/delivery/roadmap-and-management.md) and [decisions/changes](../../../docs/product/evolution/decisions-and-changes.md). No analytics/MCP/personal skills required; supplied files/reports can provide data.

- For pilots, bound audience, value outcome, access, support, risks and continuation/return criteria. Planning is not launching. Local products may have local pilots without public hosting.
- Prepare proportional operations: configuration, availability, appropriate logs, backup/restoration, rollback, maintenance and support. Cost/capacity need scenarios; do not claim demonstrated SLOs/recovery without proof.
- For growth, choose a limited audience/message/channel experiment: action, authorized investment, window, metric and review condition. Do not invent conversion, retention, CAC or campaign results. Messaging/publication/purchases need specific authorization.
- For feedback/errors/metrics, record source, context, version and impact. Distinguish episodes from trends and opinions from evidence. Research/challenge uncertain facts underlying a change.
- Classify next entry as feature, fix, design, architecture or research; read [workflows](../../../.kit/WORKFLOWS.md). Update only affected consumers and superseded decisions with reasons. Do not restart discovery for every maintenance request.

Output: pilot/operations/experiment plan or change package with base, expected result, evidence, priority and next consumer. Review delivery-related knowledge; retain history and correct links without perpetuating false information.

## Organization and results

Read authoritative sources and any selected work relevant to this request. For a simple edit, preserve existing records; do not create work/state or load process guides just to apply the skill. Consult [organization](../../../.kit/ORGANIZATION.md) when destinations/authority need clarification, [catalog](../../../.kit/CATALOG.md) when selecting/materializing a missing artifact, and [cycles](../../../.kit/WORK_CYCLES.md) when managing work state, resumption or preserved evidence. Models guide format, not observed results; materialize only what is needed and preserve existing knowledge/proofs.

For meaningful changes, update capability history with date, IDs, effect, base/revision and originating work. Use [delivery notes](../../../docs/product/delivery/release-notes.md) when consolidating delivery: distinguish unreleased from actually available changes, verify audience/version/date and dependencies, and link proof/current docs. Do not dump commits, invent per-capability versions or publish automatically.
